Five U.S. States

  1. California
    1. California is a state located on the West Coast of the United States. Its capital is Sacramento, and its largest city is Los Angeles. California is known for its technology industry, entertainment industry, diverse geography, and national parks. It became the 31st state on September 9, 1850.
  2. Hawaii
    1. Hawaii is an island state located in the Pacific Ocean. Its capital and largest city is Honolulu. Hawaii is known for its tropical climate, volcanic landscapes, beaches, and Polynesian culture. It became the 50th U.S. state on August 21, 1959.
  3. Delaware
    1. Delaware is a state in the Mid-Atlantic region of the United States. It borders Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and the Atlantic Ocean. Delaware was the first state to ratify the United States Constitution on December 7, 1787.
  4. Pennsylvania
    1. Pennsylvania is a state located in the northeastern United States. Its capital is Harrisburg, and its largest city is Philadelphia. Pennsylvania became the second state to ratify the United States Constitution on December 12, 1787.
  5. New Jersey
    1. New Jersey is a state located in the northeastern United States. It borders New York, Pennsylvania, Delaware, and the Atlantic Ocean. New Jersey became the third state to ratify the United States Constitution on December 18, 1787.
